About us.

Strata consolidates the requirements of all Stakeholders in one Comprehensive Solution.

We design, fund, build and operate the infrastructure — fibre reticulation, bulk gas, solar and storage, metering integration — and run it under a single ten-year master service agreement with the developer and the HOA. Residents deal with one provider through an official estate-branded app, verify their own consumption against the billing meter, and the HOA receives one consolidated reconciliation each month.

Levy enforcement at the meter

Smart metering allows for all costs to be charged against a Home Owner's account, which means collections can be regulated and the HOA no longer has to worry about collecting outstanding bills.

One number for every fault

Wi-Fi, gate, power, water or gas — the same contact person handles all of it. No triage between multiple providers, no dispute about whose fault it is, one line of accountability for an HOA.
