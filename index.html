01 / Overview

Estates normally buy access control, connectivity, power and metering from four or five separate suppliers, each with its own contract, hardware and support line. Strata Systems consolidates all of it behind one bulk meter and one platform.

We fund the infrastructure — fibre reticulation, bulk gas, solar panels and storage, metering integration — and operate it under a ten-year master service agreement with the developer and the HOA. Residents deal with a single provider through the Strata Systems app, verify their own consumption in real time, and pay 25% less for electricity than the municipal tariff. At the end of the term the HOA can extend the agreement or take first refusal on the infrastructure at market value.

02 / Service pillars
01

Access control

Facial and licence-plate recognition at the gate, visitor codes generated in the app, and time-bound access with audit trails for contractors and staff.

02

Fibre

A fibre-to-the-home backbone designed, built and reticulated by Strata, with the ISP service provided in-house and benchmarked against third-party pricing.

03

Solar and power

Estate-wide solar across home and clubhouse roof space with high-capacity storage, load and time-of-use management, and 24-hour standby power.

04

Water and gas

Bulk LPG tanks reticulated to every home, smart metering for water, gas and electricity, and full reconciliation and billing back to the HOA.

What the HOA gets

Levy enforcement at the meter

Where a homeowner falls behind on levies, the HOA can instruct us to restrict or cut supply to that home. Because every utility runs through our metering, enforcement does not depend on a third-party supplier agreeing to act.

One number for every fault

Wi-Fi, gate, power, water or gas — the same contact person handles all of it. No triage between four providers, no dispute about whose fault it is, one line of accountability back to the HOA.
